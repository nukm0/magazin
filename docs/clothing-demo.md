# Clothing demonstration and owner tax receipts

`?preview=1&example=clothing` loads an additive, idempotent clothing example when the active preview profile is owner. The same action is available in Owner profile -> Test clothing shop. Existing catalog and history are preserved. Fixed UUID namespace avoids duplicate examples; a workspace flag prevents replay. No real payments, messages or identities are used.

Dataset: 5 categories, 10 advertisements, 3 custom characteristics, size/color variants, Unsplash image URLs, 12 monthly receipts, 432 orders across approximately one year, customer profiles, visit counts, seller-specific category rates, product rate overrides, seller earnings, approved quarterly audits, premiums and monthly expenses. Current stock reconciles with receipts minus consumed orders and approved losses, and is fully allocated to active test sellers. Archived order costs and payout quotes are fixed. Demo quantities do not imply actual merchant stock. Photo subjects and product specifications are illustrative.

Supply tax now has separate accrual and receipt (`tax_paid_at`, `tax_paid_by`). Only owner may confirm receipt, once. Other supply actions remain available to deputy. Owner profile tax card shows receipts and opens category/time statistics. Accrual uses receipt document date; cash receipt uses confirmation timestamp; outstanding is all unpaid batches through today. Demo marks 11 batches paid and leaves one awaiting confirmation.

Paid-stock sales use calculated FIFO allocation by receipt date and accepted order timestamp (fallback creation timestamp), matching product and variant. Approved audit shortages/damage and upheld transfer write-offs consume inventory in the same event sequence. Pending delivery consumes lots but is not a sale. Only completed orders count toward sales speed. Days-to-sale is quantity-weighted from receipt to delivery; daily speed uses calendar days in selected period. This is an estimate without physical batch tracing. Unknown linkage is exposed, not silently treated as a paid batch. Payment confirmation does not charge tax again at sale.

CRM Customers and Expenses now read the shop workspace. Existing valid browser expense records are preserved and combined with workspace expenses in financial reports; new expenses are shared within the preview snapshot. Extended features still use browser persistence and test role switching; no backend multiuser tax ledger or Telegram dispatch is enabled by this example.
